# Sub Zero Ice Cream — Brand Voice Guide

## Brand identity

**Sub Zero Ice Cream** makes premium, customizable ice cream frozen on-the-spot with liquid nitrogen. It appeared on Shark Tank, which lends instant credibility. The brand has two audiences: **ice cream lovers** (consumers) and **potential franchisees** (entrepreneurs).

## Tone

| Quality | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| Casual & friendly | Write like you're talking to a friend, not a press release |
| Playful & witty | Natural wordplay on "sub-zero," "frozen," "nitrogen," temperature |
| Experiential | Describe the moment — the sight of the nitrogen cloud, the creamy texture |
| Entrepreneurial | Confident, opportunity-forward, proof-driven when speaking to franchisees |
| Accessible | Ice cream is for everyone — never elitist or overly premium-sounding |

## Vocabulary to use

- "made fresh, right in front of you"
- "your perfect scoop" / "build your own"
- "the nitrogen effect" / "nitrogen magic" / "liquid nitrogen cloud"
- "creamy, smooth, instant" (texture language)
- "proven system" / "Shark Tank-tested" (franchise angle)
- "your flavor, your way"
- "the Sub Zero experience"

## Vocabulary to avoid

- Corporate jargon ("synergy," "leverage," "solution")
- Overly scientific language (don't over-explain the nitrogen process)
- Condescending simplicity ("even you can do this")
- Competitors by name

## Content pillars

### 1. Customization & flavor creation
The endless combinations angle. Customers feel ownership over their creation.
> Example: "Strawberry cheesecake base. Graham cracker crumble. Fresh strawberries. That's *your* scoop — nobody else's."

### 2. The liquid nitrogen experience
The visual spectacle is the hook. Describe the moment.
> Example: "Watch your ice cream come to life in a cloud of nitrogen vapor. It's science. It's art. It's dessert."

### 3. Quality & indulgence
Premium ingredients, freshness, creaminess.
> Example: "No pre-made tubs. No shortcuts. Just fresh, creamy ice cream frozen the moment you order it."

### 4. Fun & celebration
Birthdays, events, date nights, after-school treats.
> Example: "Birthday parties just got a whole lot cooler. (Literally.)"

### 5. Innovation & seasonal specials
New flavor combos, trending flavors, limited-time specials.
> Example: "This week only: Brown Butter Bourbon Pecan. Get in."

### 6. Franchise success stories
Franchisee spotlights, multi-location growth, owner testimonials.
> Example: "Meet Sarah — she opened her first Sub Zero in 2021. Two locations later, she's not slowing down."

### 7. Business opportunity
Why Sub Zero is a smart franchise investment. Speak to aspiring entrepreneurs.
> Example: "Low overhead. High theatre. A product people line up for. Sub Zero franchise opportunities are open now."

### 8. Shark Tank credibility
Use the appearance as social proof without being repetitive.
> Example: "Shark Tank saw it. Thousands of customers proved it. This is the real deal."

## Platform-specific tone calibration

| Platform | Tone shift |
|---|---|
| Twitter/X | Most compressed. Lead with the hook. One idea per post. Wit over warmth. |
| Instagram | Most experiential. Describe the moment. Emoji okay. Hashtag-heavy. |
| LinkedIn | Most entrepreneurial. Franchise angle fits naturally here. Longer, narrative. |
| Facebook | Most community-focused. Ask questions. Share stories. Direct CTAs. |

## Do / Don't quick reference

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Lead with the sensory experience | Lead with a price or discount |
| Use present tense for urgency | Use passive voice |
| Mention the nitrogen spectacle naturally | Over-explain the science |
| Speak to franchisees with confidence and data | Use vague promises |
| Vary post themes across a calendar | Repeat the same angle back-to-back |
